Assumes a fixed-rate, fully amortizing 30-year loan with equal monthly principal-and-interest payments and no extra payments or refinancing. Mortgage insurance uses standard program terms: conventional PMI at the rate you enter until the balance reaches 78% of the price; FHA 1.75% upfront plus 0.55% a year (0.50% with more than 5% down), for 11 years with 10% or more down and otherwise for the life of the loan; VA funding fee of 2.15% first use or 3.3% after (1.5% with 5% down, 1.25% with 10% down), none if exempt. Program fees, FHA loan limits and PMI pricing change and vary by lender, county and credit. Leaves out property taxes, homeowners insurance, HOA dues and closing costs.
